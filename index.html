* Our Foundation & Mahabharata Heritage

Academics Rooted in Character & Dharma

Named after King Parikshit of the Mahabharata — the noble seeker of ultimate wisdom. We nurture intellectual sharpness alongside ethical integrity and mental equanimity.

Beyond Examination Ranks: Cultivating True Leaders for Life

At Pariksit, our educational philosophy is dedicated to the timeless teachings of Lord Krishna. We believe that genuine education does not merely train the intellect to solve numerical equations, but empowers the student with moral courage, mental composure, and selfless focus.

Drawing inspiration from the Bhagavad Gita and the Mahabharata, we guide students through the intense pressures of JEE and NEET with a steadfast mind (*Sthitaprajna*), transforming competitive preparation into a noble journey of personal mastery.
